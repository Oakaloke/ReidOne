About ReidOne

Journey Together

We're a faith ministry that believes no one should walk the road of faith alone. Here's who we are and why we do what we do.

Our Mission

Sharing the hope of the gospel — together

ReidOne exists to make the love of God known and the journey of faith shared. Through teaching, devotions, audio, and authentic community, we help people take the next faithful step, wherever they are on the road.

We believe faith grows best in relationship — with God and with one another. That's the heart of “Journey Together.”

What We Do

Faith for the whole journey

Three ways we come alongside you on the road of faith.

✦

Teach the Word

Clear, grace-filled teaching through sermons, devotions, and articles you can trust.

♥

Build Community

Spaces to connect, pray, and grow — because we were never meant to journey alone.

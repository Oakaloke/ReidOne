The Book of Mark

Mark tells the story of Jesus at full pace, the Gospel where things happen "immediately" and the Son of God presses on toward the cross. With urgency and power he heals the sick, frees the oppressed, stills the storm, and calls ordinary people to follow. Yet his glory is hidden until, on a Roman cross, a centurion confesses what Mark has been showing all along: "Truly this man was the Son of God." It is good news for anyone weary of religion that has lost its heart.
